Write is a calm, fast word processor for letters, articles, school work and documents. It opens and saves .docx documents as well as RTF, ODT, Markdown and plain text, and saves the finished document as a PDF with one click.

WRITE IN SEVERAL LANGUAGES
• Spelling for English, Russian, Armenian, Georgian and Azerbaijani is built in — nothing to download.
• Mix languages in one paragraph: each word is checked against the dictionary for its alphabet.

DICTATE
• Press Dictate and speak: your words appear in the document as you talk, with a brief glow that shows where the text is going.
• English, Russian and Ukrainian, recognised by macOS.

EVERYTHING A DOCUMENT NEEDS
• Fonts, paragraphs, lists, styles, tables, pictures, headers and footers, page numbers.
• Find and replace right in the side pane.
• A paper texture on screen for comfortable reading — never in PDFs or prints.

TWO WINDOWS SIDE BY SIDE
• Narrow Write to half a MacBook Air screen: the ribbon and the page adapt, and the main buttons stay where they are.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
• No accounts, no ads, no data collection. Your documents stay on your Mac.
